# Deposits Monthly Business Review (MBR) — Executive Leadership Team(ELT) Deck

> **Note on Data:** All financial metrics, balance figures, and customer data in this repository are **illustrative sample data** created for portfolio presentation purposes.

## Executive Summary
This repository contains executive-level presentation materials designed for an Executive Leadership Team (MBR review, August 2026 cycle). It evaluates portfolio growth, channel mix, pricing competitiveness, operational bottlenecks, and balance sheet concentration risks.

While total deposits grew to **$12.60B (+1.6% MoM, +9.8% YoY)**, the analysis demonstrates that **growth quality and channel asymmetry** are the core strategic challenges. Oaken (direct consumer) outperformed plan (+2.0%), while Broker lagged plan (-2.3%) due to rate positioning and lower retention.

## Key Deck Highlights

* **Portfolio Performance:** Total deposits reached $12.60B. Net New Money (NNM) rebounded to +$165M (+35M capitalized interest), narrowing the plan gap from -$230M in July to -$120M in August.
* **Channel Divergence:** 
  * **Oaken ($4.10B):** Driven by a high-yield tiered savings launch (+$62M in 3 weeks), bringing savings mix to 31.7%.
  * **Broker ($8.50B):** Lagged plan (-2.3%) as 1-year GIC rate rank dropped to 7th of 10 peers, pushing broker renewal rates down to 79% (vs. 83% target).
* **Operational Root Cause:** A vendor migration in KYC/ID verification dropped Straight-Through Processing (STP) to 71% and increased customer complaints (+22%), creating onboarding friction.
* **Funding & Balance Sheet Risks:** 
  * Top-3 broker concentration reached 41% (exceeding the 40% policy limit).
  * $2.9B in GICs mature within 90 days, requiring proactive retention strategies.

## Requested ELT Decisions & Strategy

The presentations outline four actionable decisions to close the -$120M plan gap and stabilize funding quality:

1. **Broker Pricing Adjustment:** Approve a targeted +10 bps rate action on 1–3 year Broker GICs (capped at $250M for 6 weeks, ~$0.25M annual cost) to elevate market rank to ~4th of 10.
2. **Operations Remediation:** Allocate $1.2M to fix KYC workflow bottlenecks, restoring STP to $\ge$75% and reducing complaints by 25% by November.
3. **Concentration Guardrails:** Adopt formal broker limits (top-1 $\le$ 15%, top-3 $\le$ 35% within 12 months) managed via monthly ALCO reporting.
4. **Renewal Campaign:** Launch targeted outreach on $950M maturing in September to achieve an 84% renewal target (retaining ~$24M vs. baseline).
